What this shows and what it cannot: a release date before Copilot, a named author and an unchanged
file are good evidence that people wrote the code. Simpler code-completion tools existed earlier, and
nobody can prove how any code was typed.

ADAPTED FROM A PERSON'S PAGE BY AN AI ASSISTANT

- index.html: the page's structure. It is the Album example above (item 7), changed for this list by
  the assistant; it is not an unchanged copy.
  Kept from the example: the order of the page (menu that folds open, bar with the name, title block,
  album of cards in a grid, footer with "Back to top"), and the tags and Bootstrap class names of those
  parts.
  Changed: the words (only "About" and "Back to top" are the example's); green and white instead of gray; the example's placeholder pictures and its
  small style block are gone; the example's nine cards became one card that List.js copies.
  Added by the assistant, from Bootstrap's own parts (radio button group, range, select, progress bar,
  alert, badges, list group): the category bar, the budget and sort controls, the counter of bought
  gifts, the warning line, and the inside of the card (photo, prices, notes, two buttons).
  Compare index.html with lib/album-example.html to see exactly what differs.

NOT COPIED: WRITTEN BY AN AI ASSISTANT

- app.js, all of it (about 530 lines, 70 of them the gift list): the gift list itself; the rules of
  the filter, the budget and the sort (List.js carries them out); what a card shows about its price and when it turns red; the
  "I bought this" button and its shared list; the loading and checking of the tracked prices; and the
  lines that switch the libraries above on (their settings, the confetti colors).
- tools/prices.mjs and .github/workflows/prices.yml: the hourly price tracker.
- The instructions and labels on the page. (The product names are the makers'.)

No ready-made library was found that does these parts for this list and its store, so they were not
replaced.
